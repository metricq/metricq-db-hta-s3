# Ingest and query performance of the development database, 2026-10-14

A repetition of [perf-2026-10-07](perf-2026-10-07.md) with `3058d37`, after
the compaction redesign of the past week (repack instead of consolidation and
level locality, zstd with recompression in compaction, merge-and-cut into
full single-stream objects, catalog tree packs, orphan sweep). Same laptop
(AMD Ryzen 7 PRO 4750U, 8 cores, 29 GB RAM, WAL on the internal SSD), same
stack (RabbitMQ, local RustFS and the database in Docker), same tools and
parameters; the 2026-10-07 columns below are its last runs (`f33c232` for
ingest, `8827b66` for queries). The database had been restarted 15 minutes
before the query runs; its layout was being rebuilt into full objects
(fragmentation ratio about 2.3, about 1350 objects, the load metrics' history
still in sections of about 2 MB).

## Ingest

| Scenario | 2026-10-07 | 2026-10-14 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Catch-up after a 10-minute stop: about 603,000 queued messages, 1 sample each, about 1000 metrics, prefetch 400 | 25,000-28,000 samples/s, queue empty after 31-35 s | **27,100 samples/s**, queue empty after 31-35 s |
| Six metrics, 500-sample chunks, 1 million samples each, prefetch 400 | 343,641 samples/s | **430,697 / 406,478 samples/s** (two runs) |
| same, prefetch 2000 | 401,243 samples/s | **416,936 / 417,026 samples/s** |

Catch-up ([catchup.csv](perf-2026-10-14/catchup.csv)): the first samples
arrived 4.4 s after the start (6.5 s before), then the database ingested
669,500 samples in 24.7 s at a constant rate; the RabbitMQ API updates queue
depths every 5 s, so the drain time is known to about ±2 s. This is the
production-like case, and it is unchanged: one sample per message makes it a
message rate.

The chunked runs ([ingest-chunks.csv](perf-2026-10-14/ingest-chunks.csv),
[second run](perf-2026-10-14/ingest-chunks-run2.csv)) use
`TestIngestThroughput` (integration tag, batch mode) with its own engine
against a separate RustFS, started for the run, without hold or compaction.
At prefetch 400 both runs are 18-25 % above 2026-10-07, at prefetch 2000 they
are equal. Run-to-run variation was about ±10 % before, so the gain at 400 is
small evidence at best; nothing in the ingest path changed on purpose.
